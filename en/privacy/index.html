Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

1 TL does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data.

  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app makes no network requests.
  • On-device data only. Your progress, scores, coins and settings are saved on your device and are deleted when you delete the app. You can also reset them from Settings → Progress.
  • Notifications. If you allow them, the app schedules local reminders on your device. Nothing is sent to us or any third party.
  • Children. The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.
