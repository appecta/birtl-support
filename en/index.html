1 TL is an arcade of offline games: play solo or challenge friends on the same phone. No ads, no accounts, no Wi-Fi needed.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. Everything works fully offline.

Is my progress saved?

Progress, scores and settings are stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes them. You can also reset them in Settings → Progress.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No ads and no accounts. One small price includes every game.

How do I play with a friend?

Open a 2-player game, put the phone on the table and play head to head on the same screen.

A game does not behave correctly. What should I do?

Email us your device model, iOS version and a short description so we can fix it.
